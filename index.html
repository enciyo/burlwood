What makes it different

No ads, no tracking

Nothing interrupts the game and no data leaves your device. It works fully offline.

Daily Puzzle

One 60-piece puzzle a day, the same pieces for everyone. Keep your streak and share your result.

200 Adventure levels

Each level has its own board and goals. Clear lines to collect amber, leaves, acorns, blossoms and pebbles.

Hold and Rotate

Set a piece aside in the hold slot, or turn it with a Rotate charge when the board gets tight.

Combos and cross clears

Clear a row and a column with one piece for a big bonus, and keep clearing to build combos.

Color-blind friendly

Red-green and blue-yellow palettes with grain patterns keep every block easy to tell apart.

How to play

  1. Drag a block from the tray onto the 8×8 board.
  2. Fill a whole row or column to clear it.
  3. The game ends when none of your pieces fit, so plan a few moves ahead.
